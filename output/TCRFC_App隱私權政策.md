## 中文版

台中磐石足球俱樂部（以下簡稱「本俱樂部」）重視您的隱私與個人資料保護。本政策依《個人資料保護法》說明本俱樂部如何透過官方行動應用程式「台中足球」（以下簡稱「本 App」）蒐集、處理、利用及保護您的個人資料。本 App 為台中磐石足球俱樂部與台中藍鯨女子足球隊之共同官方 App，由本俱樂部開發及營運，本俱樂部為本 App 個人資料之管理者。

最後更新日期：〔上線日期〕

一、適用範圍
本政策適用於本 App（iOS 與 Android 版）。您於本俱樂部官方網站使用之服務（例如官方商店、電子報及網站 Cookie），適用官方網站之隱私權政策。
本政策不適用於本 App 開啟之外部網站或第三方服務（例如官方網站之入會頁面、地圖與導航應用程式、電話撥號），亦不適用於由台灣足球策略發展協會主辦之慈善捐款平台；上述網站與服務各自適用其隱私權政策。

二、蒐集之目的
本俱樂部基於下列特定目的蒐集、處理及利用您的個人資料：
（一）〇五二 會員管理：會員帳號建立、身分驗證、電子會員卡、會籍與會員權益之查詢、登入裝置之管理。
（二）〇六九 契約、類似契約或其他法律關係事務：課程與試訓報名、球衣登記等服務之履行。
（三）〇九〇 消費者、客戶管理與服務：通知中心訊息、開賽提醒、回覆您的詢問。
（四）一五七 調查、統計與研究分析：統計本 App 內廣告版位之曝光與點擊次數，以提供廣告主成效報表；分析當機、啟動時間與連線錯誤，以改善 App 之穩定性。
（五）一八一 其他經營合於營業登記項目或組織章程所定之業務：球隊營運、學院與課程管理、球迷活動、會員抽獎及獎品發放。

三、蒐集之個人資料類別
依您使用的功能不同，本 App 可能蒐集下列資料：
（一）會員帳號：姓名、電子郵件、手機號碼（選填）、密碼（經雜湊處理，本俱樂部無法得知原文）、出生日期、語言偏好。
（二）未滿十八歲會員：法定代理人（父母或監護人）姓名、與會員之關係及同意時間。
（三）會籍與球衣登記：會員編號、會籍類型與有效期間、球衣尺寸及領取方式；選擇寄送者，另含收件人姓名、電話與地址。
（四）課程與試訓報名：學員姓名、出生日期、電話、電子郵件、法定代理人姓名與電話、報名備註。本 App 不蒐集健康聲明、病歷或其他健康資料。
（五）裝置資料：本 App 於安裝時產生之裝置識別碼（非廣告識別碼，解除安裝後即失效）、作業系統種類與版本、App 版本、語系、通知權限狀態；您允許通知時，另含推播服務核發之推播權杖（加密保存，不對外顯示）。
（六）廣告曝光與點擊紀錄：本 App 內廣告版位被看到或點擊之時間、廣告素材，以及上述裝置識別碼、作業系統與 App 版本、語系。此紀錄不含會員編號、完整 IP 位址、定位座標或廣告識別碼，原始紀錄保存 90 天後刪除，之後僅保留不可識別個人之彙總數字。
（七）診斷資料：本 App 發生當機或異常結束時之錯誤類別與程式堆疊、冷啟動所花時間、連線伺服器失敗時之連線路徑樣板與錯誤代碼，以及上述裝置識別碼、作業系統與 App 版本。診斷資料不含您的姓名、聯絡方式、會員編號、登入憑證或錯誤訊息全文，僅傳送至本俱樂部之系統，不經第三方分析服務，保存 90 天後刪除。
本 App 不使用廣告識別碼（IDFA／AAID）、不接入第三方廣告聯播網、不做跨 App 或跨網站追蹤，也不依您的個人資料投放廣告。

四、位置資訊
本 App 僅於您使用「特約店家」功能並允許定位權限時，取得手機的位置，用以計算您與店家之間的距離並依距離排序。距離計算全程於您的手機上完成，**位置資訊不會傳送至本俱樂部或任何第三方，也不會被儲存**。您可隨時於手機設定關閉定位權限，店家清單仍可正常瀏覽，僅不依距離排序。本 App 不在背景取得位置。

五、通知
（一）開賽提醒：由本 App 依您追蹤的球隊與賽程，於手機上排程提醒，不需將您的資料傳送至伺服器。您可於設定中關閉提醒或設定靜音時段。
（二）推播通知：本 App 透過 Apple Push Notification service（iOS）或 Firebase Cloud Messaging（Android）傳送推播，為此須將推播權杖交由上述服務提供者處理，可能涉及境外傳輸。您可隨時於手機設定關閉通知權限。
（三）通知中心之訊息保存 90 天。

六、儲存於您手機上的資料
為讓您在沒有網路時仍可使用，本 App 會在手機上保存：登入憑證（存於系統安全儲存區：iOS 鑰匙圈、Android Keystore 加密）、電子會員卡、已載入之賽程、您閱讀過之新聞（保存 30 天、最多 200 篇）、目前選擇的俱樂部、追蹤的球隊、提醒與靜音時段設定。上述資料僅存於您的手機，目前選擇的俱樂部與提醒設定不會同步至您的帳號。解除安裝本 App 即一併刪除。

七、利用之期間、地區、對象及方式
（一）期間：自蒐集之日起，至蒐集之特定目的消失、您請求停止或刪除，或依法令或契約所定之保存期限屆滿為止；廣告曝光與點擊原始紀錄、診斷資料及通知中心訊息，依前述各條保存 90 天。
（二）地區：本俱樂部所在地及本俱樂部委託之服務提供者所在地。本 App 所連線之系統架設於雲端服務供應商位於日本之資料中心，並透過網路安全服務商提供內容傳遞與資安防護；推播權杖由 Apple 或 Google 處理。因此您的資料可能於中華民國境外儲存或傳輸。
（三）對象：本俱樂部及經授權之工作人員；為完成服務而委託之第三方，包括雲端主機與資料庫、網路安全與內容傳遞、推播通知（Apple、Google）、電子郵件寄送等服務提供者；依法令要求之主管機關或司法機關。廣告主僅能取得不可識別個人之彙總成效數字。本俱樂部會要求受託者依法保護您的資料，不會將您的資料出售或出租予他人。
（四）方式：以自動化或非自動化方式，於上述目的範圍內進行蒐集、處理、利用及國際傳輸。

八、公開揭露之資料
（一）電子會員卡驗證頁：任何人掃描您的會員卡 QR Code 時，僅會看到姓名第一個字、會員編號、會籍類型及是否有效，不會顯示其他資料。您可重新產生 QR Code，使舊的驗證連結失效。
（二）會員抽獎：中獎名單僅於本俱樂部官方網站最新消息公布，內容為抽獎編號、會員編號及遮罩後之姓名（例如王○明），不會公布完整姓名或聯絡方式。

九、您的權利
依《個人資料保護法》第三條，您得就您的個人資料行使下列權利：
（一）查詢或請求閱覽；
（二）請求製給複製本；
（三）請求補充或更正；
（四）請求停止蒐集、處理或利用；
（五）請求刪除。
您可於本 App「會員」分頁自行修改資料、登出所有裝置或刪除帳號，亦可透過本俱樂部官方網站「聯絡我們」頁面提出申請。本俱樂部於確認您的身分後儘速處理；依法令須保存之資料（例如交易與發票紀錄）於保存期間內無法刪除。

十、不提供資料之影響
您可自由選擇是否提供個人資料。不登入亦可瀏覽賽程、新聞、球隊與特約店家；若您未提供必要欄位之資料，本俱樂部可能無法為您建立帳號、顯示電子會員卡、受理報名或球衣登記。

十一、帳號刪除
您可直接於本 App 內刪除帳號：iOS 版為「會員 → 帳號 → 刪除帳號」，Android 版為「會員 → 會員資料與帳號 → 刪除帳號」。刪除後，本俱樂部將清除帳號之個人資料、使電子會員卡失效並登出所有裝置。依法令須保存之訂單與付款紀錄，以及抽獎名單中之會員編號與遮罩姓名，將於保存期間內保留。未登入之裝置資料，於您解除安裝本 App 後即無法再與您連結。

十二、未成年人個人資料保護
未滿十八歲者註冊會員，須經法定代理人同意，並由法定代理人提供其姓名及關係。學院、課程及試訓報名之學員多為未成年人，本俱樂部僅於報名與活動管理之必要範圍內利用其資料。本 App 之學院與課程畫面不放置廣告。

十三、照片與影像
本 App 所顯示之球員與教練照片，僅於取得肖像同意（未成年者為法定代理人同意）後顯示。如您不希望特定影像繼續公開，請透過本俱樂部官方網站「聯絡我們」頁面告知。

十四、資料安全維護措施
本俱樂部採取下列措施保護您的個人資料：本 App 與伺服器間全程以 HTTPS 加密傳輸；密碼經雜湊處理；登入憑證存於手機系統安全儲存區；推播權杖加密保存；會員卡 QR Code 無法由會員編號推導；後台依職務分級授權，未經授權之人員僅能看到遮罩後之資料；個人資料之檢視與匯出均留有稽核紀錄；資料定期備份。

十五、政策修訂
本俱樂部得因法令變更或服務調整修訂本政策，修訂後之內容將公布於本頁面，並標示最後更新日期。重大變更時，本俱樂部將另以 App 內通知或電子郵件通知。

十六、聯絡方式
對本政策或您的個人資料有任何疑問或請求，請透過本俱樂部官方網站「聯絡我們」頁面與本俱樂部聯繫，或來信〔俱樂部聯絡信箱〕。

## English

Taichung Rock FC (the "Club", "we" or "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains, in accordance with Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act, how we collect, process, use and protect your personal data through our official mobile app, Taichung Football (the "App"). The App is the joint official app of Taichung Rock FC and Taichung Blue Whale Women's Football Club. It is developed and operated by the Club, and the Club is the controller of the personal data processed through the App.

Last updated: [date of publication]

1. Scope
This policy applies to the App on iOS and Android. Services you use on the Club's website (such as the official shop, the newsletter and website cookies) are covered by the website's privacy policy.
It does not apply to external websites or third-party services opened from the App (such as the membership pages on the Club's website, map and navigation apps, and phone calls), or to the charity donation platform operated by the Taiwan Football Strategic Development Association. Those websites and services have their own privacy policies.

2. Purposes of collection
We collect, process and use your personal data for the following purposes:
(a) Membership management: creating accounts, verifying identity, digital membership cards, viewing memberships and member benefits, and managing signed-in devices.
(b) Contractual and similar relationships: providing course and trial registrations, jersey registration and other services.
(c) Customer management and service: notification centre messages, kick-off reminders and responding to your enquiries.
(d) Statistics and analysis: counting impressions and clicks on in-app ad placements, to give advertisers performance reports; and analysing crashes, start-up times and connection errors, to improve the App's stability.
(e) Other activities within the Club's registered business or articles: team operations, academy and course management, fan events, member prize draws and prize delivery.

3. Categories of personal data
Depending on the features you use, the App may collect:
(a) Member account: name, email address, mobile number (optional), password (stored as a hash, so we cannot see it), date of birth and language preference.
(b) Members under 18: the name of the legal guardian (parent or guardian), their relationship to the member, and the time of consent.
(c) Membership and jersey registration: member number, membership type and validity period, jersey size and collection method. If you choose delivery, the recipient's name, phone number and address.
(d) Course and trial registrations: the participant's name, date of birth, phone number and email address, the guardian's name and phone number, and registration notes. The App does not collect health declarations, medical records or any other health data.
(e) Device data: an installation identifier generated by the App when it is installed (not an advertising identifier; it stops working when you uninstall the App), operating system and version, App version, language and notification permission status. If you allow notifications, the push token issued by the push notification service (stored encrypted and never displayed).
(f) Ad impression and click records: when an in-app ad placement was seen or tapped, which ad it was, and the installation identifier, operating system, App version and language described above. These records do not contain your member number, full IP address, location or any advertising identifier. Raw records are deleted after 90 days; only aggregate figures that cannot identify anyone are kept after that.
(g) Diagnostic data: when the App crashes or exits abnormally, the error type and stack trace; how long a cold start took; and, when a connection to our servers fails, the path template and error code, together with the installation identifier, operating system and App version described above. Diagnostic data does not contain your name, contact details, member number, sign-in credentials or the full text of error messages. It is sent only to the Club's own systems, not to any third-party analytics service, and is deleted after 90 days.
The App does not use advertising identifiers (IDFA/AAID), does not use third-party ad networks, does not track you across other apps or websites, and does not target ads based on your personal data.

4. Location
The App accesses your phone's location only when you use the Partner Stores feature and have granted location permission, to calculate the distance to each store and sort stores by distance. The calculation happens entirely on your phone. **Your location is never sent to the Club or to anyone else, and it is not stored.** You can turn off location permission at any time in your phone's settings; the store list still works, just without sorting by distance. The App does not access your location in the background.

5. Notifications
(a) Kick-off reminders are scheduled on your phone based on the teams you follow and their fixtures; no data needs to be sent to our servers. You can turn them off or set quiet hours in Settings.
(b) Push notifications are delivered through the Apple Push Notification service (iOS) or Firebase Cloud Messaging (Android). Your push token is processed by these providers for this purpose, which may involve transfer outside Taiwan. You can turn off notifications at any time in your phone's settings.
(c) Messages in the notification centre are kept for 90 days.

6. Data stored on your phone
So that you can use the App without a connection, it stores the following on your phone: sign-in credentials (in the system's secure storage: the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore encryption), your digital membership cards, fixtures that have been loaded, news articles you have read (kept for 30 days, up to 200 articles), the club you have selected, the teams you follow, and your reminder and quiet-hours settings. This data stays on your phone; your selected club and reminder settings are not synced to your account. It is deleted when you uninstall the App.

7. Period, region, recipients and manner of use
(a) Period: from the date of collection until the purpose of collection no longer exists, you ask us to stop using or to delete the data, or the retention period required by law or contract expires. Raw ad impression and click records, diagnostic data and notification centre messages are kept for 90 days as described above.
(b) Region: the location of the Club and of the service providers we engage. The systems the App connects to are hosted in a cloud provider's data centre in Japan, and content delivery and security are provided by a network security provider. Push tokens are processed by Apple or Google. Your data may therefore be stored or transferred outside Taiwan.
(c) Recipients: the Club and its authorised staff; third parties engaged to provide our services, including cloud hosting and databases, network security and content delivery, push notifications (Apple, Google) and email delivery; and government or judicial authorities where required by law. Advertisers receive only aggregate performance figures that cannot identify anyone. We require our service providers to protect your data in accordance with the law. We do not sell or rent your data to anyone.
(d) Manner: collection, processing, use and international transfer, by automated or non-automated means, within the purposes above.

8. Information made public
(a) Membership card verification page: anyone who scans your membership card QR code sees only the first character of your name, your member number, your membership type and whether it is valid. You can regenerate the QR code, which disables the old verification link.
(b) Member prize draws: winners are announced only in the News section of the Club's website, showing the draw number, member number and masked name (for example, 王○明). Full names and contact details are never published.

9. Your rights
Under Article 3 of the Personal Data Protection Act, you may:
(a) enquire about or review your personal data;
(b) request a copy;
(c) request that it be supplemented or corrected;
(d) request that we stop collecting, processing or using it;
(e) request that it be deleted.
You can update your details, sign out other devices or delete your account yourself in the Member tab of the App, or contact us through the Contact Us page of the Club's website. We will act on your request promptly once we have confirmed your identity. Data we are required by law to keep, such as transaction and invoice records, cannot be deleted during the retention period.

10. If you do not provide your data
Providing personal data is voluntary. You can browse fixtures, news, teams and partner stores without signing in. If you do not complete the required fields, we may be unable to create your account, show your digital membership card, or accept your registrations or jersey registration.

11. Account deletion
You can delete your account directly in the App: on iOS, Member > Account > Delete account; on Android, Member > Profile & account > Delete account. When you do, we erase the personal data in your account, deactivate your membership cards and sign you out on all devices. Order and payment records required by law, and the member number and masked name in prize-draw rosters, are kept for the applicable retention period. Device data from devices that are not signed in can no longer be linked to you once you uninstall the App.

12. Minors
A person under 18 may register only with the consent of their legal guardian, who must provide their name and relationship to the member. Most participants in academy, course and trial registrations are minors; we use their data only as needed to manage registrations and activities. The App does not show ads on academy and course screens.

13. Photographs
Photographs of players and coaches are shown in the App only after portrait consent has been obtained (for minors, from their legal guardian). If you do not want a particular image to remain public, please let us know through the Contact Us page of the Club's website.

14. Security
We protect your personal data by: encrypting all traffic between the App and our servers with HTTPS; hashing passwords; keeping sign-in credentials in your phone's secure system storage; encrypting push tokens; making membership card QR codes impossible to derive from member numbers; limiting back-office access by role, so that unauthorised staff see only masked data; keeping audit logs of every view and export of personal data; and backing up data regularly.

15. Changes to this policy
We may update this policy because of changes in the law or in our services. The updated policy will be posted on this page with the date of the latest update. For significant changes, we will also notify you in the App or by email.

16. Contact
If you have any questions or requests about this policy or your personal data, please contact us through the Contact Us page of the Club's website, or email [Club contact email].
