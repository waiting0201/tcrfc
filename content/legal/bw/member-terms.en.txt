These terms apply to member accounts, memberships, digital membership cards and related member services provided through the Club's official website and official mobile app.

(a) You may register with an email address or a LINE account. If you register by email, you must verify your email address before using member services.

For family plans, the main account holder fills in the details of family members and is responsible for their accuracy.

4. Membership types
(a) Registered member: free, and granted on registration. Gives access to basic services such as the Member Centre and the digital membership card.
(b) Fan club member: granted on payment of the annual fee, with the benefits set out in the plan, such as discounts at partner stores, a member jersey, priority for fan events and entry into member prize draws.
The fee, period, benefits, number of membership cards and number of jerseys of each plan are as stated in the plan description published on this website or in the official app at the time you apply.

5. Membership period and renewal
(a) A membership lasts one season. All memberships for the same season expire on the same date, whenever they were taken out. Memberships taken out mid-season are charged as stated in the plan.
(b) We will email you 30 days before your membership expires and again when it expires. If you do not renew, your paid benefits end and your account remains as a registered member.

6. Fees and refunds
(a) If you apply to upgrade through the website, your membership is activated once we have confirmed payment. If you pay in the official app, your membership is activated automatically when payment is complete.
(b) Refunds of membership fees follow the rules stated in the plan you applied for. Jerseys already collected and benefits already used are not refundable or creditable. If a fee is refunded, the membership and its benefits are cancelled.
(c) Membership fees are collected on the Club's behalf by Taichung Rock FC, which also issues the e-invoices.

7. Digital membership card
(a) Each membership comes with one digital membership card, showing the club crest, member number, QR code, name, membership type and expiry date.
(b) The card is for your personal use only and may not be transferred or lent. Partner stores and event staff may scan the QR code to verify it. The verification page shows only the first character of your name, your member number, your membership type and whether it is valid.
(c) If you think someone else has your QR code, you can regenerate it in the Member Centre and the old QR code will stop working immediately.

8. Member benefits
(a) Partner-store discounts, including their content, period and conditions, are provided by each partner store as announced. We may change the list of partner stores according to our partnerships.
(b) Member jersey size registration and collection follow the arrangements announced each season. If you do not register or collect your jersey by the announced deadline, you are deemed to have given it up.
(c) Places at fan events are limited, and priority access does not guarantee a place.

9. Member prize draws
(a) While your membership is valid, you are automatically included in the list of members eligible for fan member prize draws. If you win, your name will be announced in masked form in the News section.
(b) You do not need to enter separately. Members of both Taichung Blue Whale and Taichung Rock FC may take part in both clubs' draws.
(c) The prizes, eligibility, dates and prize collection of each draw are set out in the rules for that draw.
(d) If the value of a prize reaches the income-tax withholding threshold, the winner must provide their national ID number and registered address as required by law, and is responsible for any tax due. A winner who does not provide them is deemed to have given up the prize. Prizes won by minors are handed to their legal guardian or to the main account holder of the family plan.

10. Official app
(a) If you turn on push notifications in the official app, we store your device's push identifier and send notifications through Apple's and Google's push services, whose servers may be located outside Taiwan. You can turn off notifications at any time in your device settings.
(b) When you register for a course or event as a member, the registration is linked to your member account so that you can view it in the Member Centre.
(c) Features such as "Nearby partner stores" use your location only on your device to calculate distances. Your location is never uploaded to us.
